1. Problem Statement & Core Innovation

The Problem: Existing health apps overwhelm users with dozens of disconnected graphs and generic chatbots that give template advice and forget previous answers. Information entered during onboarding is not shared across food, movement, and progress.

The Solution: HEALTH PLUS is built as ONE connected, living AI wellness companion:

  • Understands Before Prescribing: An 8-stage clinical onboarding interview discovers lifestyle, regional Indian cuisine, kitchen setup, budget, allergies, and health conditions.
  • Clinical Safety & Grounded Nutrition: Targets use Mifflin-St Jeor formulas, 1200 kcal clinical floors, hypertension sodium bounds, and renal protein ceilings.
  • Connected 5-Pillar Architecture: Today, Food Hub, Move Studio, AI Coach, and Progress all share the exact same user state and memory in real time.
  • Natural Hinglish Voice Companion: STT/TTS with automatic language detection, rejection reasoning, and pantry memory.

2. Complete System Workflow

USER VISITS LANDING PAGE (/) ↓ AUTHENTICATION GATEWAY (/login, /signup, or 3 Pre-Seeded Demo Accounts) ↓ CONVERSATIONAL AI ONBOARDING (8 Adaptive Clinical Stages) ↓ CLINICAL TARGETS GENERATION (BMR, TDEE, Safe Macro Floors) ↓ CONNECTED 5-PILLAR EXPERIENCE: ├── [TODAY] : 1 Calm AI Summary Line + 1 Clear Next Action + Hydration + Streak ├── [FOOD HUB] : Safe Recipes + Derived Macros + Daily Indian Plan + Grocery Checklist ├── [MOVE STUDIO] : 3D Kinematic Human Pose Avatar + Guided Form Cues + Rep Counter ├── [AI COACH] : Hinglish Voice Companion + Rejection Reasoning + Pantry Substitution └── [PROGRESS] : Multi-Day Baseline Corridor + Rolling Averages + Rep Charts

6. Judges' Tough Questions & Ready Winning Answers

Q1: "Is the AI hallucinating nutrition numbers, or are they verified?"
Answer: "Every recipe has explicit gram portions and derived macros computed through our deterministic ingredient composition formula (foodComposition.ts). Daily targets use Mifflin-St Jeor clinical equations enforcing a strict 1200 kcal starvation floor and 1500mg sodium limit."
Q2: "What happens if there is no internet connection or no LLM API key?"
Answer: "The app never crashes. We built a hybrid dual-mode architecture: if an external LLM key is absent, our client-side deterministic NLU engine (aiOrchestrator.ts) takes over seamlessly, handling language continuity, rejection reasoning, duration parsing, and nutrition math completely offline."
Q3: "How does the AI handle Indian diets and local ingredients?"
Answer: "Western apps suggest avocado and quinoa. HEALTH PLUS is built specifically for Indian lifestyles: homestyle regional cuisines (North, South, Gujarati, Bengali), staples like Dal, Poha, Ragi, Besan, Paneer, and factors in hostel kettles or gas stoves."
Q4: "Where is user health data stored? Is it private?"
Answer: "We follow a local-first privacy architecture. User data resides in isolated per-user browser vaults (persistenceService.ts). Password authentication uses 64-byte salted scryptSync hashing resistant to timing attacks. No user data leaks between accounts or demo profiles."
